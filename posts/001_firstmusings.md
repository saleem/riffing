# Preamble

I was laid off in July 2026. The reason was what's called a "reduction in force" event, where an organization downsizes its headcount to remove fixed costs from its balancesheet.

That's such a mouthful! I was fired, along with more than a dozen other people, because employees cost money. And firing employees is a gauranteed way to save money, especially when the organization is desperate to save money.

This is an emotional experience like no other. That's not to say it's the worst thing that can happen to a person; there are obviously worse calamities – some of which have brushed me personally and will probably find mention in future posts. As I deal with this challenge, I figured I should share my thoughts, feelings, and actions with others in similar situations. At a minimum, my hope is to create a connection with you who're reading this. I also hope that others in similar situation may find something of emotional support or practical value in this.

## What's in a name?

I called this "Riffing" as play on words: RIF is the event that has caused my current joblessness. Also, given the nature of my current thoughts, it's likely that these writings are likely to appear as a riff than a scholarly treatise. That's what I'm gunning for.

## Who is this for?

These musings are primarily for people who have also been laid off work and are looking to regain employment.

If you're already employed, retired, financially secure, or somehow hermetically insulated from the daily aggravation of making a budget and transacting in money to survive – you may find my writing less useful. You're still welcome to read it, of course.

## What to expect in the future

I am actively looking for a job, so I am likely to share my thoughts on that experience.

I'm also actively trying to retain my mental health. I will almost certainly share what I've done, and what I've failed or succeeded at, in my headspace.

I may also digress into sundry observations about our society, our economy and its principles, the impact of LLMs on our lives, and other broad topics which are both popular and controversial.

My purpose is to be curious, introspective, and transparent. It's neither to cajole or provoke. I am responsibe for my words. Your reactions to them are wholly yours.
